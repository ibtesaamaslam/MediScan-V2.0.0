# Clinical User Personas - MediScan Local

## Persona 1: Fatima - The Rural Community Health Worker (CHW)
*Fatima is a local field health organizer deploying medical assistance across remote regions with limited grid access, sparse cellular tower service, and direct sunlight exposures.*

*   **Age:** 29
*   **Location:** Thar District, Sindh
*   **Primary Device:** Budget Android Smartphone (6GB RAM, Quantized Snapdragon processor)
*   **Daily Gaps / Constraints:**
    *   No stable cellular data index when carrying out local home inspections.
    *   Finds it difficult to read low-contrast screen UI components when under intense direct sun glare.
    *   Manages up to 30 sequential patients per single cluster session.
*   **Systems Needs:**
    *   We need a prominent, highly readable, light-background high-contrast layout.
    *   Immediate offline data writing with quick patient ID registration.
    *   A guided, tactile camera crop viewfinder overlay with clear visual instructions.

*Dr. Aisha is a consulting Board-Certified Dermatologist operating from a central healthcare clinic. She reviews incoming referred screening payloads that are synchronized once the CHW nodes re-establish network connectivity.*

*   **Age:** 45
*   **Location:** Jinnah Hospital, Karachi
*   **Primary Device:** High-spec Desktop Station & Tablet
*   **Daily Gaps / Constraints:**
    *   High patient volumes require quick auditing logs.
    *   Wants to immediately inspect exact model confidence bounds and Platt calibration ranges.
    *   Requires clean PDF diagnostics formatting templates for HIPAA archival logs.
*   **Systems Needs:**
    *   Structured historical details with exact timestamp arrays.
    *   Clinical error reporting detailing which exact version of the ONNX Model performed the evaluation.
    *   Single-click PDF download capabilities.

## Persona 3: Omar - Rural Field Patient
*Omar is a local agriculture worker experiencing a progressive skin lesion. He is wary of entering major central city clinics because of transport cost constraints and daily loss of agricultural wages.*

*   **Age:** 42
*   **Location:** Village Hub, Rural Sindh
*   **Daily Gaps / Constraints:**
    *   Speak Urdu as primary dialect.
    *   No experience with digital healthcare machinery.
    *   Recommends immediate explanation tools during guided scan sessions to feel secure.
*   **Systems Needs:**
    *   Urdu RTL translation layouts fully integrated with local text-to-speech support.
    *   Simplified, clear medical severity statuses (Clear emerald, amber, and coral flags).
    *   Clear privacy/consent disclosures in local dialects before scanning procedures begin.
