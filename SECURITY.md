## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of MediScan Local seriously. If you believe your finding is a security vulnerability, please do NOT open a public issue. Instead, report it to our clinical security response team by emailing **clinical-security@mediscan.local**.

Please include:
- A detailed description of the vulnerability.
- Steps to reproduce or a working Proof of Concept (PoC).
- The potential impact on patient data privacy or clinical decision accuracy.

We aim to acknowledge your report within 24 hours and provide a detailed response with a remediation timeline within 72 hours.
